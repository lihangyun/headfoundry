# ADR0002: do not promote local capacity gains into photo reconstruction

Date:2026-10-14. Status: accepted **experiment/stop policy**, not accepted geometry.

Public-product research suggests global priors plus local residuals, but earlier
HeadFoundry mouth/contour experiments did not establish a reliable remedy. Use
one fixed dense3D oracle experiment before any new subject fitting. Implementation
must remain independent, opt-in and use the already licensed asset chain.

Experiment0208 implements64/192 mesh-geodesic patches and improves all12 same-GNM
oracle cases numerically, safely under the implemented checks. Its existing30-D
oracle baseline was already within subpixel mean projection error, and actual
five-view white meshes do not show the sought identity-level leap. Therefore:

1. Retain the minimal helper and reproducible diagnostic, **not a default path**.
2. Freeze its parameters; no further node/ridge/amplitude sweeps on these cases.
3. Separate representation coverage from observation/camera reliability. Require
   distinct legally cleared known3D evidence and measured correspondence noise
   before another expensive subject fit. Independently generated AI images are
   design references, not common-camera/physical3D validation.
4. Keep `TECHNICAL_CHECK_PASSED`, narrow synthetic `PARTIAL_SUCCESS`, real-reference
   `UNVERIFIED` and promotion `REJECT` separate. Nothing here is `ACCEPT` or proof
   of KeenTools parity. Surface normal/area checks do not certify self-intersections
   or anatomical correctness.

Consequence: avoid expanding a proven-to-compute but not proven-to-help shape
system. No camera gate waiver, predictor approval, training, paid asset adoption,
private reverse engineering, texture/hair work or default change is introduced.
See [the complete experiment](../experiment-0208-dense-3d-local-capacity.md).

## 2026-10-17: distinguish missing observation from representation capacity

0210's free patch registration and0213's new local-rigidity screen both miss the
30% cross-source gain gate; the latter stops after one case without rendering or
strength/iteration sweeps. A read-only0213 audit finds16.17% of scoped source area
distance-trimmed but bearing93.66% of remaining forward vertex-quadrature squared
error. Those points are held toward the frozen prior rather than the target.
This is not the sampled symmetric acceptance score or proof of anatomical
correspondence. Do not attribute the whole failed gate to insufficient shape
capacity, and do not simply remove trimming or reduce the evaluation scope.

Before another subject fit or deformation-field variant, localize and verify
whether ignored geometry is comparable head anatomy or source/target neck,
aperture or domain mismatch. Keep the four original development targets, full
evaluation scope and prior defaults unchanged. New observation evidence must be
separate from repeating the frozen field's weights/masks under another name.

0211's native-mean hidden-contact audit also invalidates calling that selected
row an observed lip seam. 0212's failed refined-surface transfer demonstrates
that edited-source coefficients cannot be assumed safe on native construction;
it does not prove all native identity fitting impossible. Neither finding
authorizes another closure sweep, gate relaxation or head-quality acceptance.

## 2026-10-18: preserve useful coverage evidence without promoting boundary failure

Input-only coverage images localize a real posterior cranial omission separately
from neck-cut mismatch. Exact normal-line observations recover posterior extent;
removing unsupported-position pseudo-data further lowers proximity errors.
0214/0215 achieve58.92%/72.46% on the original mean target, but actual full-head
views reveal neck ridge/curling. Both are visually rejected and frozen after one
case. The four-case cross-source gate remains unpassed; no defaults may change.

Do not sweep rest weights or add successive terms to this same boundary setup.
The next useful research target is reliable person-specific facial observation,
with a clearly bounded local-domain hypothesis if justified by evidence. Existing
AI-image limitations, semantic visibility checks and whole-head visual review
remain. A localized trial must preserve surrounding geometry and disclose its
experimental scope; it cannot relabel these failed full-head registrations as
accepted or stand in for final recognizable-head delivery.

## 2026-10-19: real paired-source calibration before further shape changes

Two user-authorized real video/skin-scan/bone-scan cases now have local hash-bound
intake, decoded-frame evidence and actual source-only white-mesh previews
(0216). This supports moving from independently generated references to a
paired-source development pilot. It does not yet establish units, acquisition
stage equivalence, scan accuracy, camera alignment or a recovered head.

Develop calibration on case001 and reserve case002 from parameter fitting until
the method is frozen. Explicitly disclose scan-assisted alignment versus a
photo-only pipeline, and keep video frame holdouts out of fitting. Inspect
nose/cheek/chin projection and scan defect regions before a new shape candidate.
Closed scan eyes, posterior artifacts, cap-covered video scalp and artificial
neck closures cannot silently become full-head surface acceptance targets.
