# Experiment 0143: FLAME Open identity transfer

Status: asset/conversion path `TECHNICAL_CHECK_PASSED`; identity and visual
promotion `REJECT`.

This experiment changes only the identity prior. Experiment-0132 cameras,
photographs and protected measurements remain fixed. The hypothesis is that the
official 300-dimensional FLAME 2023 Open space can transfer the current subject
shape into a standard, coherent topology and improve side-profile fidelity. The
counter-hypothesis is that model availability alone supplies only a generic face
and cannot represent the subject from the current evidence.

The official model initially required a coordinate conversion from FLAME's
y-down/z-forward convention to the project's y-up/z-back convention. The first
sign-only trial was upside down and is preserved as an excluded invalid result.
The corrected rigid convention and trimmed similarity alignment produce a
coherent head, neck and eye topology without changing the input cameras.

Three bounded routes were then tested. A 100-direction photo fit improves the
generic FLAME mean numerically but remains visibly generic. A shared object
similarity improves the held profile errors while worsening central and some eye
evidence. Finally, all 300 identity directions were fitted to the existing 0132
surface as a topology-transfer initialization. That transfer reduces trimmed
surface distance from 0.02831 to 0.02683, keeps minimum triangle-area ratio
0.7624 and produces zero tested normal reversals. Its bilateral 90-degree
profile means improve only from 33.60/31.80 px to 32.22/30.42 px. The current
0132 result remains much better at approximately 3.62/4.92 px.

Actual five-view renders confirm the numerical result: the FLAME mean and
transfer are nearly indistinguishable and retain a generic face, including the
wrong nose, lips, chin and cranial outline. Therefore the installation and
safe-decoding path are `TECHNICAL_CHECK_PASSED`, but every tested identity
candidate and default promotion is `REJECT`. These are fit-set diagnostics, not
independent likeness evidence, and KeenTools-level quality remains `UNVERIFIED`.

Next gate: do not tune the same linear FLAME identity fit again. Retain FLAME as
a legal standard topology/rig prior only. A future subject-identity route needs
new independent shape evidence or a more expressive free-commercial prior, and
must beat experiment 0132 on bilateral profiles and protected central/eye
evidence before visual review or texture work.
